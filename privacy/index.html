Privacy

Last updated September 15, 2026

In shortThis website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Prometheus runs on your own computer, and Aethra Intelligence does not receive your files, your requests or your results.

This website

aethraintelligence.com is a static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

The Prometheus app

  • Your work stays on your computer. Files, results, history and what Prometheus remembers are stored on your own machine.
  • No account. Prometheus does not ask you to sign up, and Aethra Intelligence does not receive your data.
  • AI providers are your choice. With local AI models, requests are processed on your computer. If you connect a hosted AI provider, the text of each request is sent to that provider under its own privacy terms.
  • The web and connected services. When Prometheus searches the web or uses a service you connected, such as email or cloud storage, it communicates with those sites and services on your behalf.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@aethraintelligence.com.
